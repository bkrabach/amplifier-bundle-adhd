# Scenario: Plan a config migration (Priya)

Your team's mini-service at `/root/project/fixtures/miniapp/` reads
`config.json` in two modules. Ops wants everything on YAML by Friday. It's
2:30pm; your medication window closes around 4. You need a plan you can put
on a calendar, then you'll start the first step today.

## How to run this session

1. In the agent TUI, first type `/adhd` to activate ADHD mode.
2. In your direct, economical voice, describe the migration and ask for a
   plan FIRST — explicitly ask "how long, in minutes, per step?" You intend
   to schedule this.
3. Evaluate the plan: is it capped/ranked (today vs later)? Are estimates
   in concrete minutes with honest uncertainty, or vague?
4. Tell it to start step 1 (only step 1) and watch whether it stays bounded.
5. Simulate an interruption: send "back from a 1:1, where are we?" and
   evaluate the re-orientation.
6. Ask "how long for what's left?" — check whether the estimate stays
   concrete and consistent with reality so far.
7. Let it finish step 2 if time permits, then wrap up. Keep the whole
   interaction under 10 exchanges.

## What to evaluate (carry this into your conclude summary)

- Time estimates: concrete minutes with honest uncertainty, or vague filler? Quote the best/worst.
- Plan shape: capped at ~5, ranked, today-vs-later split? Or ten unranked steps?
- Boundedness: did "do step 1 only" stay step 1 only?
- State restating: after the interruption, did one glance tell you step N of M and what's next?
- Consistency: did later estimates match earlier ones as work progressed?
- Trust: as a tech lead, would you recommend this mode to your team (including the two engineers you suspect have ADHD)? Why/why not?
- Verdict on the scenario task itself, plus the ONE change that would most improve it.
